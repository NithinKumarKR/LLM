Earth is a planet in our solar system that's home to life. It's the third planet from the sun and the fifth largest planet in our solar system. 
Physical characteristics
Shape: Earth is a sphere with a diameter of 7,917.5 miles 
Composition: Earth is made of rock, metals, and silicate rocks 
Atmosphere: Earth's atmosphere is made of 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and 1% other gases 
Magnetic field: Earth's magnetic field is generated by convection currents in the outer core 
Motion 
Rotation: Earth rotates on its axis every 23.9 hours, which defines day and night
Orbit: Earth orbits the sun every 365.25 days, which defines the year
Other facts
Earth is known as the blue planet because of its large amount of water 
Earth's highest mountain is Mount Everest, which is part of the Himalayan mountain range 
Earth's surface is constantly changing due to convection cells inside the planet 
Earth's atmosphere protects us from the sun's radiation and most meteoroids
